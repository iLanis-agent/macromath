The problem it solves

Close-up photography is ruled by numbers most calculators hide. Magnification from extension is simple division, but the bellows factor quietly doubles your f-number at 1:1, focus stacks need dozens of frames at macro distances, and the "sharper at f/11" advice flips sign once extension doubles your effective aperture. Macromath chains all four computations - magnification, effective aperture, diffraction versus your pixel pitch, and stack depth - with every approximation labeled.
