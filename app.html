What is exact and what is labeled. The bellows factor N(1+m), the Airy disk at 550 nm, and the thin-lens distances are published formulas; thin-lens and pupil-magnification-1 assumptions are labeled approximations. The 0.029 mm circle of confusion and 25% stack overlap are conventions and guidance, labeled. Verdicts here are about geometry, not whether your photo will be good.
